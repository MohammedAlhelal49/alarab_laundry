Construction Costing Application

End-to-end costing management from CRM Lead to Project Delivery

This module extends Odoo with a structured construction job costing workflow integrated with CRM and Projects. It captures client requirements at the CRM lead level and transforms them into a structured Job Cost Project.

Key Features

  • Detailed Job Cost Sheets
  • Materials, Labor & Equipment Costing
  • Risk and Margin Calculations
  • State-driven Workflow Control
  • Project & Milestone Generation
  • Weight-based Progress Tracking
  • Multi-duration Equipment Costing
  • Sequential Locking of Sheets

Integration Workflow

CRM Lead Job Cost Project Job Cost Sheets Odoo Project & Tasks

Designed for construction and contracting businesses requiring precise cost control, structured execution, and full data integrity through strict validations and constraints.
