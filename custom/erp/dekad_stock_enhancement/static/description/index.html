Dekad Inventory Cost

Set product cost directly from the Physical Inventory screen

What does it do?

This module adds a Cost column to the Physical Inventory screen, right next to the counted quantity. Enter the new cost while you count your stock, click Apply, and the product's Cost is updated automatically — the same way it would be if you edited it on the product form, with full support for Average Cost (AVCO) valuation and accounting entries.

Once this module is installed, the Cost field on the product form becomes read-only: all cost updates go through Physical Inventory, so there is always a single, consistent place to update product costs.

Key Features

Cost column

A new editable Cost column appears right after Counted Quantity on the Physical Inventory list.

Total column

A new Total column shows On Hand Quantity × Cost, with a sum row at the bottom of the list for a quick overview.

Single source of truth

The Cost field on the product form becomes read-only. Cost changes can only be made from Physical Inventory.

AVCO compatible

Stock valuation layers and accounting journal entries are generated automatically and correctly, exactly as Odoo does when you change the cost from the product form.

How To Use

  1. Open Inventory > Operations > Physical Inventory.
  2. Enter the counted quantity as usual.
  3. Enter the new cost in the Cost column.
  4. Click Apply (or Apply All).
  5. The product's Cost is updated automatically. You can verify it on the product form (read-only) or under Inventory > Reporting > Valuation.
Note: if the same product appears on several inventory lines (different locations or lots) with different cost values, Odoo will block the Apply with a clear warning until all lines for that product share the same cost.
